# Aderir à WOO - Produto Móvel

urls = ["https://appsmobileqms.nos.pt/WOO_MOB/PreLogin","https://appsmobileqms.nos.pt/WOO_MOB/IJoin"]

## Steps

1. Launch browser and navigate to url 'https://appsmobileqms.nos.pt/WOO_MOB/PreLogin'
2. Verify that the pre-login page is visible successfully
3. Click on the 'Change Language' link at the top of the page to open the language menu
4. Select 'Portuguese' as the app language and click the 'Confirm' button
5. Verify that the page is now in Portuguese: the 'Aderir à WOO' button is visible
6. Click on the 'Aderir à WOO' button
7. Verify that the 'Produtos WOO' page is visible successfully
8. Verify that the 'Móvel' product option is visible (the mobile-only option, not 'Móvel + Net Fixa' or another bundle)
9. Click on the 'Móvel' product option
10. Click the 'Aderir já' button of the first mobile plan in the list
11. Verify that the 'Escolhe o tipo de cartão' section is visible
12. Select the 'Físico' card type and click the 'Continuar' button
13. Verify that the 'Escolhe a morada' section is visible
14. Verify that the postal code ('código postal') field is visible: the text field with the hint 'Escreve o teu código postal.'
15. Type the postal code '4100-304' in the postal code field and click the search button next to it
16. Choose the morada (street) 'Rua ZONA_R QMS' from the list of addresses found
17. Choose the porta (door number) '449' from the list that appears next
18. Choose the alojamento (floor and side) '16 Direito' from the list that appears next
19. Click the 'Confirmar morada' button
20. Verify that the 'Preenche os teus dados' section is visible
